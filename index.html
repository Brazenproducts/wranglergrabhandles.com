Why grab handles matter on a Wrangler

The Jeep Wrangler's roll bar is exposed the moment you pull the doors off or drop the top. On a trail, passengers need something solid to grip when the terrain gets rough. The factory grab handles are minimal — and most Wrangler owners either upgrade immediately or realize too late they should have.

The two main options are paracord and nylon/aluminum. Paracord handles (invented by Bartact) wrap the entire roll bar, stay grippy in mud and rain, and don't get hot in summer the way aluminum does. Nylon handles are lighter and cheaper. Aluminum handles are solid but rigid — better for step assist than trail gripping.

Generation matters too. JK handles don't always fit JL without modification. Always confirm fitment for your specific year before buying.
