Paracord vs nylon vs aluminum

The three main materials each have a use case. Paracord (Bartact — the original, widely copied) wraps the roll bar and stays grippy wet or dry. Nylon strap handles are cheaper and fine for casual use. Aluminum is rigid — better for step assist than trail gripping.
